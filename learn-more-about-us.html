Meet Our Team

Executive Director & Founder

Anthony Smith, Ph.D.

Licensed Psychologist · Certified Health Services Provider, North Carolina

Dr. Smith is the Executive Director & Founder of ACE Transitions. He has been involved in the mental health field for over 25 years. He is a Licensed Psychologist and Certified Health Services Provider in North Carolina. He previously served on the faculty at the Duke University Medical School as a professor of Behavioral Science.

He also has prior experience with the Duke Employee Assistance Program (EAP), providing a host of psychological services including short-term individual and couples therapy, crisis management, managed care gate-keeping (including authorization of psychiatric hospitalization) and case management, fitness for duty evaluations, and psychological testing.

Dr. Smith has also worked in a Federal and State prison, a Veterans Affairs hospital, and a University Counseling Center. This broad training allows him to work effectively with a wide variety of mental health-related problems.

Education

  • Ph.D. and M.A. in Counseling Psychology, Southern Illinois University at Carbondale
  • B.A., University of Notre Dame

Professional Involvement

  • Association of Black Psychologists
  • American Psychological Association
  • North Carolina Psychological Association

He previously served on the board of the North Carolina Psychological Association for three years, and as president of the Triangle Chapter of the Association of Black Psychologists.

Program Coordinator

Nancy Lloyd

Certified North Carolina Peer Support Specialist · Certified North Carolina Drug and Alcohol Counselor

With over 15 years of experience serving the community, I am passionate about helping individuals overcome challenges, rebuild their lives, and discover their full potential. As a Certified North Carolina Peer Support Specialist and Certified North Carolina Drug and Alcohol Counselor, I am dedicated to supporting individuals navigating mental health challenges, substance use recovery, and life transitions.

Throughout my career, I have had the privilege of working with individuals from diverse backgrounds, including eight years of service as a Case Manager within Mental Health Court. This experience strengthened my commitment to advocacy, accountability, recovery, and helping justice-involved individuals access the resources and support they need to move forward.

I believe that every person deserves compassion, dignity, respect, and an opportunity for a fresh start. My approach is rooted in understanding, empowerment, and the belief that a person’s past does not define their future.

My mission is to help individuals recognize their strengths, overcome barriers, build resilience, and create meaningful, lasting change. Through community service, peer support, and recovery-focused guidance, I strive to be a source of encouragement and hope for those working toward a healthier, more stable, and productive life.

My purpose is simple: to meet people where they are, remind them of what is possible, and help them move toward where they want to be.

Compliance Manager

Kristi Riley

U.S. Army Veteran · Program Manager, City of Durham Welcome Home Program · Qualified Professional, ACE Transitions

Kristi Riley is a proud U.S. Army veteran, dedicated professional, and passionate community leader. She currently serves as the Program Manager for the City of Durham’s Welcome Home Program and as a Qualified Professional (QP) for ACE Transitions.

Education

  • Associate degrees in Paralegal Studies
  • Bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Legal Studies
  • Bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity, concentration in Digital Forensics
  • Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Cybersecurity

With extensive experience in leadership, case management, and community support, Kristi is committed to empowering individuals, creating opportunities, and making a lasting impact through advocacy, service, and professional excellence.
